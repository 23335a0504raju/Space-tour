## 5. Creative constraints 🔒

1. Only verified personal information; placeholders until approved.
2. Every scene must deliver **one** idea.
3. In space, the Sun is the only key light source; its intensity follows distance.
4. Text appears at rest points, never during fast motion.
5. One accent colour in the UI; monochrome otherwise.
6. Resume and contact are reachable from **every** moment of the experience.
7. All content exists in an accessible, non-3D form (Mission Brief).
8. Scientific accuracy where practical; cinematic license on distances and speed, never on facts presented as facts.
9. Projects are always human-made structures; identity and knowledge are always natural bodies.
10. No scene ships without a mobile framing plan.
