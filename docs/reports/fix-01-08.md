## Revision after review (2026-11-18): room, asteroids, black hole fall
User feedback: the room still looked nasty, the asteroids were not fine, and the fall into the black hole and the way out must look real, like Interstellar.
- **Black hole (SC10 and SC12):** real gravitational lensing as a screen-space pass (`shaders/lens.ts`, first in the post chain, also on Low). The Einstein ring grows as the camera approaches, bending the stars and the ship into arcs around the hole. The fall itself is now real rendering: darkness, sparse red-shifted and blue-shifted star streaks, and a light at the far end that grows (`createFallMaterial`). The ink/cel re-draw no longer happens in the fall: in SC10 it happens as the ship breaks out into the light (`styleAt` 0.74 to T_EXIT); in SC12 the cel world un-draws as the ship enters the hole. Lens state is a pure function of the camera position, so scrolling back is exact.
- **Asteroids (SC07):** darker albedo (charcoal and umber), three cratered hero shapes (9000-triangle Bennu with 38–54 power-law craters and a raised rim), 36 mid rocks (2500 triangles, cratered) and small rocks, harder contrast. Triangles: desktop 412k, Low 172k (budgets 600k / 200k).
- **Room (SC02, SC03):** the mismatched retro props are gone (wooden leather armchair, metal drawer desk, shelf that read as a ladder, ceiling globe, orange lamp). New code-built white crew desk with a lit edge and drawer unit, and an ergonomic crew chair with casters (`room/Furniture.tsx`); flush ceiling light panels, dark skirting, calmer deck grating.
- **Black hole opening (follow-up):** the hole no longer fills the screen at the start. SC10 opens with a small distant black hole ahead of the ship (`holeRadiusAt`: horizon 3 to 16 m over t = 0 to 0.42), so you watch it grow as you scroll, lens the ship into arcs, then take it at t = 0.5. SC12 mirrors it (6 to 16 m over t = 0 to 0.25). The horizon radius is a pure function of t, so scrolling back shrinks it exactly.
- **Flicker and blank frames in the fall (fixed):** found by sweeping t with a frame-mean probe. Root cause: the fall shader used `pow(1 - v, 14)` where `v` can land a hair outside 0..1 at the cylinder rim; `pow` of a negative base is NaN, and one NaN pixel blooms over the whole frame. That gave black frames (and, through the cel pass, an empty pink gradient), and it depended on how the camera arrived, so it looked random. Fixes: `v` is clamped, both black hole shaders discard NaN/Inf output, `atan(0, 0)` is nudged. Also: the swallow is now a cross-fade scaled by the horizon radius (a dead-black stretch around t = 0.64 to 0.7 is gone), the fall is a backdrop (`depthTest` off, drawn first) so it can never hide the ship, the painted sky and clouds appear only once the camera is well into the fall, and the star streaks are fewer and wider so they do not shimmer. Probe results (mean brightness per frame, 8 shots each): no all-black frames from t = 0.5 to 0.96 in SC10, nor from 0.05 to 0.9 in SC12; values steady across the 8 shots.
