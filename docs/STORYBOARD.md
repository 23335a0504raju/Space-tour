**Legend:**
- **Dwell**: a soft rest zone where motion slows and text is readable.
- **Rest point**: a frame that keyboard / chapter navigation lands on.

## ACT I — ORIGIN

### S00 · Countdown (loading)
- **Purpose:** turn the unavoidable asset loading into the first story beat.
- **Environment:** black screen.
- **Astronaut:** not visible.
- **Camera:** none (2D).
- **Animation:** a mission-clock-style counter tied to **real loading progress** (never fake). Small type: `MISSION [your initials] · PREPARING`.
- **Scroll:** disabled only while critical assets load (a few seconds max). **Mission Brief** and **Resume** links are available here immediately.
- **Info:** your name `[TO CONFIRM]`.
- **Transition:** the counter reaches zero → fade to a moonlit window.
- **Assets:** none (typography).

### S01 · The Window  · *Rest point 01*  · ~2 screens
- **Purpose:** hook. Establish mood and foreshadow the Moon.
- **Environment:** night sky through a bedroom window; the Moon is visible.
- **Astronaut:** off-screen at first.
- **Camera:** starts tight on the window glass (Moon reflected in it), then slowly **pulls back** into the room.
- **Animation:** curtains move slightly; monitor glow flickers on the walls.
- **Scroll:** controls the pull-back.
- **Info (dwell):** name + one-line identity `[TO CONFIRM]`, e.g. "Full-stack developer" (exact wording from you).
- **Transition:** the pull-back reveals an astronaut sitting at the desk, typing.
- **Assets:** room interior, window, night sky + Moon backdrop, astronaut (seated pose).

### S02 · The Desk  · ~3 screens
- **Purpose:** introduction and background, human and specific.
- **Environment:** the desk: two monitors (real code `[TO CONFIRM: which repo]`), books, corkboard, helmet on the desk.
- **Astronaut:** seated, typing, then pauses and looks out the window (idle → look animation).
- **Camera:** over-the-shoulder → slow orbit around the desk to frame the books and corkboard.
- **Animation:** code scrolls on screen; lamp light; dust motes in lamp beam.
- **Scroll:** drives the orbit; text lines appear at two dwell points.
- **Info:** origin and background `[TO CONFIRM]` (2–3 short lines). The book spines hint at your education field `[TO CONFIRM]`.
- **Optional interaction:** hover/tap objects for a one-line caption (only for real objects).
- **Transition:** the astronaut reaches for the helmet. **Cut.**
- **Assets:** desk props, books, monitors, corkboard items, helmet, astronaut (sit-idle, typing, look-up animations).

### S03 · Suit Up & Walk  · ~2 screens
- **Purpose:** the decision to leave. "Why I build" moment.
- **Environment:** room; doorway in background, light under the door.
- **Astronaut:** helmet now on (we never see it put on), stands up, walks toward the door.
- **Camera:** low tracking dolly behind and to the side, then rotates to face the door.
- **Animation:** walk cycle with distance-locked steps (no foot sliding); visor reflects the monitor glow.
- **Scroll:** controls the astronaut's distance along the path; reversing makes the astronaut walk back.
- **Info (dwell, short):** one sentence: why you build / what drives you `[TO CONFIRM]`.
- **Transition:** the astronaut opens the door → overwhelming white light.
- **Assets:** astronaut (stand-up, walk, door-reach animations), door.

### S04 · The Door Is the Airlock  · ~1 screen
- **Purpose:** the house → ship transition with no exterior modelling.
- **Environment:** white-out.
- **Camera:** pushes through the door frame; the frame **match-cuts** into the circular/rectangular hatch frame of the ship; light dims to cockpit tones.
- **Scroll:** linear progress through the cut.
- **Info:** none.
- **Transition:** hatch → cockpit interior.
- **Assets:** door frame and hatch frame designed to share a silhouette.

## ACT II — IGNITION

### S05 · Cockpit / Mission Profile  · *Rest point 02*  · ~3 screens
- **Purpose:** professional identity and orientation (the visitor learns how to navigate).
- **Environment:** cockpit: two seats, wide window (launch tower beyond), screens.
- **Astronaut:** climbs into the left seat, straps in (simplified: seated pose + arm reach).
- **Camera:** from the hatch → over-the-shoulder → close on screens.
- **Animation:** screens boot in sequence.
- **Info:**
  - *Mission Profile* screen: role, specialisation, location/availability `[TO CONFIRM]`.
  - *Flight Plan* screen: the list of chapters. The UI hints "This is your navigation" and the real menu glows briefly in sync.
- **Transition:** the astronaut flips the ignition switch (close-up of a gloved hand).
- **Assets:** cockpit interior, screens (rendered from HTML/canvas), astronaut seated + reach animations.

### S06 · Ignition  · ~2 screens
- **Purpose:** the first exterior reveal; energy rises.
- **Environment:** launch pad at dawn or night, exhaust plume, tower.
- **Camera:** cut to a **wide exterior** of the ship on the pad → slow push in as engines ignite.
- **Animation:** exhaust glow, smoke billowing (sprites), tower clamps release, subtle camera shake.
- **Scroll:** controls engine intensity → liftoff.
- **Info:** none (or a minimal countdown readout).
- **Assets:** ship exterior, launch pad/tower (low detail), exhaust and smoke effects, ground plane.

### S07 · Ascent  · ~2 screens
- **Purpose:** speed and altitude.
- **Environment:** cloud layers, sky gradient blue → indigo → black.
- **Camera:** tracking shot alongside the ship; then a cockpit window POV punching through the cloud deck.
- **Animation:** clouds rushing past (layered planes), condensation, stage separation optional `[DECISION]`.
- **Info:** none, or one line (mission statement) `[TO CONFIRM]`.
- **Assets:** cloud textures/sprites, sky shader, ship exterior.

### S08 · Orbital Sunrise  · *Rest point 03*  · ~2 screens
- **Purpose:** the signature "breathe" moment; scale.
- **Environment:** Earth below (night side with city lights), the Sun rising over the limb.
- **Camera:** behind the ship, then a slow rotation as the sunrise blooms; Earth shrinks.
- **Animation:** atmosphere rim glow, terminator line moving, city lights fading as daylight sweeps.
- **Info:** chapter title card: `THE SYSTEM`.
- **Transition:** the ship turns toward the Moon.
- **Assets:** Earth (day, night, clouds, specular maps), atmosphere shader, Sun lens effect.

## ACT III — THE SYSTEM

### S09 · Moon / Education  · *Rest point 04*  · ~3 screens
- **Purpose:** education as the first step beyond home.
- **Environment:** low-altitude pass over the lunar terminator (long shadows, craters).
- **Camera:** skimming flight → rises to reveal Earth over the lunar horizon ("Earthrise" composition).
- **Animation:** shadows sweeping across craters.
- **Info (dwell):** degree, institution, years and relevant focus `[TO CONFIRM]`, styled as a mission record ("Training: …").
- **Transition:** the ship turns sunward; brightness ramps.
- **Assets:** Moon surface (height-mapped terrain strip + sphere), Earth (reused).

### S10 · The Sun / Core Identity  · *Rest point 05*  · ~4 screens
- **Purpose:** the heart of the film: what you're made of.
- **Environment:** the Sun filling the frame (granulation, prominences), the ship's heat shield in silhouette.
- **Astronaut:** brief interior beat: visor reflecting the light while the instrument screen activates.
- **Camera:** locked in the shield's shadow → on the instrument display.
- **Animation:** sunlight → prism → **spectrum band**; dark absorption lines appear one by one, each labelled.
- **Info:** 3–5 core strengths/principles `[TO CONFIRM]`. Header: *"You can tell what a star is made of by its light."*
- **Transition:** **slingshot**: the ship curves around and accelerates outward, and sunlight rakes across the frame toward the outer system.
- **Assets:** Sun shader, ship heat-shield side, spectrum UI (code), cockpit reuse.

### S11 · Mars / Experience  · *Rest point 06*  · ~4 screens
- **Purpose:** professional experience as real missions.
- **Environment:** Mars from orbit, then the night side, where **outpost lights** are visible on the surface (one light cluster per role/internship).
- **Camera:** orbital approach → slow pan across the outposts; each settles into a dwell.
- **Animation:** dust haze at the limb; outpost lights pulse once as they are "contacted".
- **Info:** per outpost: organisation, role, dates and 1–2 verified responsibilities `[TO CONFIRM]`, styled as mission logs.
- **Interaction:** select an outpost for an expanded log (overlay).
- **Transition:** leave orbit → rocks begin streaming past.
- **Assets:** Mars (colour, normal, night-side tint), outpost light markers (code).

### S12 · Asteroid Belt / Other Work  · ~2 screens
- **Purpose:** breadth: smaller projects and other full-stack work.
- **Environment:** a dense field of rotating asteroids.
- **Camera:** fast fly-through with parallax; brief slow-down beside flagged asteroids.
- **Animation:** instanced rocks; 3–6 rocks carry small beacons.
- **Info:** each beacon: project name, one line and stack `[TO CONFIRM]`. Links in overlay/Brief.
- **Transition:** the field thins → Jupiter's banded disc grows.
- **Assets:** 3–5 asteroid meshes (instanced), beacon marker.

### S13 · Jupiter System / Flagship Projects  · *Rest point 07*  · ~6 screens
- **Purpose:** the deepest content: what you built.
- **Environment:** Jupiter filling the frame; 2–3 **orbital stations**, one per flagship project.
- **Camera:** wide establishing shot (stations tiny against Jupiter) → approach each station in turn.
- **Animation:** station lights, docking-light sequence on approach.
- **Info at each station (dwell):** project name, one-line purpose, your role `[TO CONFIRM]`, and a "Enter Project Bay" action.
- **Interaction:**
  - **Tier 1 station** → Project Bay opens: an interactive diorama of the project's core concept (e.g. Smart Way Traffic, only if it accurately reflects the real system) + problem / role / stack / architecture / links.
  - **Tier 2 station** → cinematic dossier with real screenshots or recordings.
  - Closing returns to the exact frame.
- **Transition:** departure burn → Saturn appears backlit.
- **Assets:** Jupiter texture, 1 modular station model (re-dressed per project), diorama assets, project media.

### S14 · Saturn / Tech Stack  · *Rest point 08*  · ~3 screens
- **Purpose:** skills, structured and readable.
- **Environment:** Saturn backlit; rings edge-on (a thin line).
- **Camera:** rises above the ring plane so the rings **open up** layer by layer.
- **Animation:** each ring band highlights in turn and is labelled: Languages → Frontend → Backend → Databases → Cloud/Deployment `[TO CONFIRM contents]`. Ring particles drift close to the lens.
- **Info:** technologies per band, grouped, with no logos (or monochrome logos only, `[DECISION]`).
- **Transition:** Saturn recedes; the Sun is now small and the light colder.
- **Assets:** Saturn + ring texture, ring particle field, labels (code).

## ACT IV — THE EDGE

### S15 · Heliopause & Voyager / Learning  · *Rest point 09*  · ~3 screens
- **Purpose:** the journey continues: current learning and aspirations.
- **Environment:** a sparse starfield; the distant Sun; **Voyager 1** drifting.
- **Camera:** very slow approach to Voyager → its Golden Record in close-up.
- **Animation:** subtle particle-direction change at the heliopause boundary.
- **Info:** "Currently learning: …" and "Aspiring to: …" `[TO CONFIRM]`.
- **Assets:** Voyager model (NASA public-domain models exist; check licence), starfield.

### S16 · Deep Space  · ~2 screens
- **Purpose:** humility and scale; a breath before the climax.
- **Environment:** the Milky Way band, nebulae, distant galaxies.
- **Camera:** ship small in the frame, slow drift.
- **Info:** one sentence `[TO CONFIRM]` or none.
- **Assets:** high-resolution sky panorama (licensing to verify) or procedural sky.

### S17 · The Wormhole  · ~1.5 screens
- **Purpose:** the threshold into fiction.
- **Environment:** a spherical lensing distortion (the *Interstellar*-style "sphere", not a neon tunnel).
- **Camera:** approach → enter → a short tunnel of bent starlight.
- **Info:** none.
- **Assets:** lensing shader (code).

## ACT V — THE UNBUILT

### S18 · The Unbuilt Universe / Future Direction  · *Rest point 10*  · ~3 screens
- **Purpose:** what you want to build next.
- **Environment:** a universe of construction lines: wireframe nebulae, grid-traced planets and dotted orbits.
- **Camera:** rising reveal; as the visitor scrolls, structures **resolve from wireframe to solid**.
- **Info:** 2–3 future directions `[TO CONFIRM]`, each appearing as a structure materialises.
- **Transition:** the ship settles near a small platform.
- **Assets:** wireframe-to-solid shader (code), reuse of existing planet meshes.

### S19 · Final Transmission  · *Rest point 11*  · ~2 screens
- **Purpose:** the invitation: easy, clear contact.
- **Environment:** the platform in the now-solid universe.
- **Astronaut:** steps out, plants the beacon, stands beside it facing the vista (stand / plant / idle animations).
- **Camera:** wide → medium; the astronaut turns slightly toward the camera.
- **Animation:** beacon powers on, sending a pulse of light outward.
- **Info (HTML layer):** name, role, **Download Resume**, GitHub, LinkedIn, Email, other contacts `[TO CONFIRM]`, plus "Replay mission".
- **Assets:** platform, beacon model, astronaut animations.
